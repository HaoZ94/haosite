# Brain anatomy

`brain-fsaverage5.json` is a **modified conversion** of the FreeSurfer fsaverage5 pial surfaces and sulcal-depth arrays distributed in Nilearn. Each hemisphere contains 10,242 vertices and 20,480 triangular faces. The signals and links between anatomical surfaces added by the renderer are illustrative, not recorded neural activity or measured neural tracts.

Source: [Nilearn fsaverage5 dataset](https://github.com/nilearn/nilearn/tree/e5faab66dc2fe72d2cb50a8aaf2f44813aba4a51/nilearn/datasets/data/fsaverage5), revision `e5faab66dc2fe72d2cb50a8aaf2f44813aba4a51`.

Inputs: `pial_left.gii.gz`, `pial_right.gii.gz`, `sulc_left.gii.gz`, and `sulc_right.gii.gz`. GIFTI arrays were decompressed and converted to JSON. Coordinates were rounded to three decimal places and sulcal depths to four; topology was preserved. Coordinates retain anatomical RAS orientation and are centered, scaled, and rotated for display by the renderer.

`brain-subcortical.json` is a **modified derivative** of the official [FreeSurfer fsaverage `aseg.mgz` segmentation](https://www.freesurfer.net/pub/dist/freesurfer/tutorial_versions_centos6/freesurfer/subjects/fsaverage/mri/aseg.mgz). The left cerebellum combines labels 7 and 8; the right combines labels 46 and 47; the brainstem uses label 16. Each mask was smoothed with a 0.75 mm Gaussian and converted to a triangular mesh with marching cubes at 2 mm sampling. The largest connected component was retained, normals were oriented outward, and coordinates were rounded to three decimal places. The volume header's `vox2ras_tkr` transform places all three surfaces in the same surface RAS coordinates as the cortex. Fine cerebellar folia are limited by the segmentation resolution.

Source volume SHA-256: `b55ef817e5e5bd1a3d76d4a2305283eef16fbff199ead3d840dd4b0fa8205e48`. The derived meshes contain 3,874 vertices / 7,744 faces (left cerebellum), 3,880 / 7,756 (right cerebellum), and 2,146 / 4,288 (brainstem). Geometry provenance is also embedded in the JSON asset.

`brain-hippocampus.json` is a **modified derivative** of that same checksummed fsaverage segmentation, using labels 17 (left hippocampus) and 53 (right hippocampus). A 0.7 mm Gaussian and marching cubes at 1 mm sampling produce closed, outward-facing surfaces in the matching `vox2ras_tkr` surface RAS coordinates. The left mesh has 3,506 vertices / 7,008 faces, and the right has 3,520 / 7,036. These represent the whole hippocampi, not individual hippocampal subfields. Source details, generator hash, dependency versions, and mesh measurements are recorded in `brain-hippocampus.metadata.json`.

Rebuild with `python3 scripts/build-hippocampus.py --source /path/to/aseg.mgz`. The script verifies the source checksum and requires NumPy, nibabel, SciPy, scikit-image, and trimesh; the exact versions used are in the metadata.

`brain-regions.json` is a **modified derivative** of the official [FreeSurfer fsaverage5 Desikan–Killiany annotations](https://www.freesurfer.net/pub/dist/freesurfer/tutorial_versions_centos6/freesurfer/subjects/fsaverage5/label/), `lh.aparc.annot` and `rh.aparc.annot`. Its two arrays each contain one integer per vertex, in the same order as the existing pial geometry. The matching official pial surfaces have identical triangle indices and agree with the stored coordinates within their 0.001 mm rounding. Source file SHA-256 checksums and the complete parcel-to-group mapping are embedded in the asset and generation script.

The display groups the atlas parcels using FreeSurfer's [approximate lobe mapping](https://www.freesurfer.net/fswiki/CorticalParcellation): `1` frontal, `2` parietal, `3` temporal, `4` occipital, `5` insula, and `6` cingulate cortex. Cingulate cortex is kept separate from the four lobes; it is not presented as the whole limbic system. Code `0` retains unknown and corpus-callosum annotations as an unassigned medial wall, not as a distinct cortical lobe. These are anatomical atlas groups, not functional networks. Some parcels span traditional lobar boundaries, so this grouping is approximate. Any separation between groups in the expanded display is illustrative and does not represent anatomical distance.

`brain-region-solids.json.gz` contains fourteen closed cortical portions. The expanded display uses these smooth surfaces for the twelve named portions and two medial walls. The medial walls remain stationary, use a uniform opaque neutral material, and have no independent labels or expanded picking targets; their exact source surfaces return on reassembly. `npm run build:brain-solids` generates the volumes from the local cortex and atlas: it adds curved illustrative interiors, fills their signed voxel union at 1.4 mm spacing, applies a 1.2 mm Gaussian, and extracts a smooth isosurface. Each result is a single closed, consistently wound volume. Projected signal anchors and normals keep interactions attached to the new surface. Input hashes, settings, and output counts are recorded in the accompanying metadata and inside the compressed asset. The generator is deterministic and requires no downloads.

The expanded cerebellum, brainstem, and hippocampi use one guarded Loop subdivision pass. Hippocampal shading normals additionally receive six averaging passes to soften small segmentation ridges. These display adjustments preserve the major folds but simplify fine details. The source meshes are unchanged, and reassembly restores their original shape. The cortical volumes' new interior faces are illustrative, not anatomical tissue segmentations.

Rebuild and validate the region asset with `python3 scripts/build-brain-regions.py` (standard library only). This downloads the four pinned source files, verifies their checksums, and checks both official surfaces against the existing mesh before writing the JSON. To reuse source files, pass `--source-dir /path/to/directory` containing `lh.aparc.annot`, `rh.aparc.annot`, `lh.pial`, and `rh.pial`.
