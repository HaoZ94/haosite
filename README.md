# Hao Zhu · 朱昊

I’m a postdoctoral fellow at the Brain and Mind Institute, in the Department of Linguistics and Modern Languages at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. I hold a B.S. and Ph.D. in Neuroscience from New York University.

I study how the brain represents language and how motor and sensory systems work together to turn intention into speech. My research uses EEG, MEG, ECoG, and sEEG to explore these processes across space and time. I’m also interested in brain–computer interfaces and their potential to restore communication.
